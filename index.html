WHY THIS CHAMPIONSHIP

Built around the coast, for the coast

Every match, cluster and category is designed around one goal: giving fishing-community youth a real stage to compete on.

01

Promote sports among coastal youth

Structured, well-organised competition brought directly into 608 coastal villages.

02

Identify talent

A group-to-finals format that surfaces the strongest players and teams from every cluster.

03

Strengthen coastal communities

A shared championship that brings villages, clusters and regions together around sport.

THE FORMAT

Six sports, three categories

Three team sports and two individual sports are each contested by every age category. Turf Cricket runs as one open bracket for all ages.

Before you register

  • Team events need a team name and a captain's contact details.
  • Individual events (Yogasana, Silambam) register one athlete at a time.
  • Turf Cricket is a single open bracket across all three age categories.
  • Your age determines your category: Junior 14–18, Youth 19–25, Senior Youth 26–35.

Need help?

Contact your cluster coordinator or reach the organising committee for registration support.
